# OneDish Devpost Thumbnail Design

## Goal

Create a high-impact English thumbnail for the OneDish Devpost submission that remains legible at small card size.

## Composition

- 3:2 landscape canvas, targeting 1500 × 1000 pixels.
- Deep near-black background with restrained lime and coral orbital light accents.
- A polished meal bowl and a compact, believable OneDish decision interface form the central visual.
- Large exact title: `OneDish`.
- Exact tagline: `The right meal, right now.`

## Visual Direction

The image should feel like a premium AI consumer product rather than a generic food advertisement. Use the project's near-black, electric lime, warm coral, and off-white palette. Preserve generous negative space and strong foreground/background separation.

## Constraints

- English only.
- No fine print, fake metrics, illegible interface copy, watermarks, or third-party logos.
- Text must be spelled exactly and remain readable when reduced.
- Export as PNG or JPG under 5 MB.
- The final asset must be stored in `docs/assets/` without overwriting the existing hero image.

## Acceptance Checks

- Pixel aspect ratio is exactly 3:2.
- File size is below 5 MB.
- Title and tagline are readable.
- Visual identity matches the current OneDish product.
